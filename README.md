# 🌍 Gaia-01
### Galileo Atmospheric Instrument Array — 01

**Getting a little closer to understanding the world around us.**

Gaia-01 is a small environmental monitoring project built around a simple idea: what if we could get a better look at the atmosphere right around us?

Using environmental sensors and an Arduino-compatible microcontroller, Gaia-01 measures the temperature and relative humidity of its surroundings, turning invisible environmental conditions into information we can actually see and understand.

### 🌡️ What does it measure?

- **Temperature** — How warm or cold is the surrounding environment?
- **Humidity** — How much moisture is present in the air?

Two simple measurements, but a fascinating starting point for exploring the environment through electronics.

### 🔬 The idea behind Gaia

The name Gaia comes from Greek mythology, where Gaia represents the Earth. Combined with Galileo Atmospheric Instrument Array, it reflects the spirit behind this project: curiosity, observation, and a little bit of science.

Gaia-01 is also the beginning of an idea that could grow much further. There is a whole world of environmental data waiting to be explored, and temperature and humidity are just the beginning.

### 🛠️ Built with curiosity

This project brings together electronics, programming, sensors, and a growing interest in robotics. It's about experimenting, learning how things work, and turning an idea into something tangible.

Because sometimes, the best way to understand the world is to start measuring it.

**Welcome to Gaia-01.** 🌍

**Hardware Needed⚙️**
- DHT11 Temperature + Humidity Sensor 
- BMP280 Barometric Pressure Sensor Module 3.3V
- 2.8inch LCD Touchscreen ESP32 CYD Module – WiFi/Bluetooth
Note: The LCD Touchscreen comes with a ESP 32 soldered to it, so no nee to buy a separate development board!
